# Code of Conduct

Vericov contributors and maintainers are expected to make participation
welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free.

## Expected Behavior

- Be considerate, specific, and constructive.
- Critique ideas and code without attacking people.
- Respect different backgrounds, identities, experience levels, and viewpoints.
- Accept feedback and correct mistakes in good faith.
- Protect private information and security reports.

## Unacceptable Behavior

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexualized conduct, sustained
disruption, doxxing, and retaliation are not tolerated in project spaces.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately through the contact path in
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Maintainers may edit or remove contributions,
temporarily restrict participation, or permanently ban participants when
necessary to protect the community. Reports will be handled as confidentially
as practical.

This policy applies in repository discussions, reviews, community spaces, and
public interactions made on behalf of Vericov.
